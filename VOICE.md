# TradeAid — Voice & Tone

This document is the source of truth for how TradeAid speaks. Everything we write
touches this file first: interface copy, transactional emails, marketing pages,
support replies, error messages, later on employee communications and hiring.

The goal is a single, recognizable voice — the reader should be able to feel
they're reading TradeAid even without the logo on the page.

---

## The core promise

**TradeAid respects the reader.**

The market is genuinely difficult. The industry sells it as easy. Our voice is
the correction — we treat learning to trade as a skill worth taking seriously,
and we treat the reader as someone capable of doing that work.

Everything else follows from that.

---

## Voice attributes

Five attributes. Each has a positive edge (what we are) and a negative edge
(what we're not). The negative edge matters as much as the positive one.

### 1. Measured, not urgent

We are — deliberate, considered, unhurried, confident. Long sentences when they
carry weight, short ones when they land.
We are not — breathless, FOMO-inducing, dopamine-farming, "act now."

- ✅ *A research-grounded curriculum on investing and trading best practices.*
- ❌ *Master the market NOW! Don't miss the biggest bull run of the decade!*

### 2. Editorial, not corporate

We are — literary, precise, warm through craft rather than through friendliness
theater. Cormorant Garamond serif exists because we care how a sentence looks.
We are not — buzzword-driven, thought-leadery, "at TradeAid, we believe…"

- ✅ *The world's pulse, without the noise.*
- ❌ *Empowering the next generation of retail investors with cutting-edge tools.*

### 3. Confident, not hyped

We are — declarative, grounded in specifics (numbers, evidence, real papers).
We are not — hedging, disclaimery beyond what's legally required, or overselling.

- ✅ *19 lessons across 3 modules. 12 papers in the library.*
- ❌ *An unbelievable, revolutionary, game-changing platform.*

### 4. Direct, not clever

We are — second person ("you"), plain nouns, active voice, one clear next step.
We are not — cutesy, punny, ironic, ambiguous, or performatively humble.

- ✅ *Practice this trade.*
- ❌ *Let's dip our toes into simulator-land!*

### 5. Honest about limits

We are — transparent that markets are risky and this is education, not advice.
We are not — hiding disclaimers, dressing simulated data as real, or promising outcomes.

- ✅ *Educational simulation only — no real order is placed, no money at risk.*
- ❌ *Start earning today.*

## Concrete rules

Written as if you're auditing a piece of copy. If it breaks a rule, rewrite it.

**Punctuation & typography**
- No exclamation points. Ever. (Confidence doesn't need them.)
- No emojis in product copy or emails. Icons yes, emojis no.
- Em-dashes for asides — used the way a good essayist uses them.
- Serif italic for emphasized phrases in headlines (color: gold-deep).
- Real quotation marks: " " and ' ', not " and '.

**Words we don't use**
- amazing, awesome, incredible, unbelievable, revolutionary, game-changing
- unlock, empower, curate, best-in-class, next-generation, cutting-edge
- our users, our members, our community (say "you" or "traders")
- crush, dominate, beat the market, get rich, easy money
- literally (unless it literally is)

**Words we like**
- practice, discipline, evidence, process, skill, patience
- measured, deliberate, considered, quiet
- research, curriculum, coaching, simulation

**Numerics**
- Concrete counts over vague scale — "19 lessons" beats "many lessons."
- Percentages to two decimals in financial contexts (+1.41%, not +1.4%).
- Currency with proper thousands separators ($4,782.15).

**Sentence shape**
- Cut the second half of any sentence that starts with "In today's fast-paced…"
- If you can delete "very" or "really" without meaning changing, delete it.
- One idea per sentence in body copy. Save the long ones for headlines.

**Titles & buttons**
- Section eyebrows: UPPERCASE, letter-spaced 0.18em, burgundy.
- Buttons: UPPERCASE, letter-spaced 0.15em, action verb — CONTINUE, GET STARTED, BUY LONG.
- Never gerund-only buttons ("Learning" instead of "Learn") unless it's a section label.

## Emails — the mode we default to

Transactional emails carry the voice into the inbox. They are the most personal
surface we have. The rules:

- **Warm opening, no exclamation.** "Welcome, Otto." not "Welcome Otto!!"
- **One idea per email.** Don't cram announcements into a welcome.
- **One clear action.** A single button, not a menu.
- **From a person, not a brand.** Signed by a name (or "The TradeAid team")
  — never "TradeAid" as if the domain wrote it.
- **Reply-to that works.** If you send it, someone reads replies.
- **Short subject lines.** 4–7 words. No emoji, no ALL CAPS, no "🎉 You're in!!"

### Welcome email — template feel

> Subject: Welcome to TradeAid
>
> Otto,
>
> Your account is ready. You've picked *hands-on* as your way in — that
> means we'll open you at the simulator, not the reading list.
>
> Take your first paper trade whenever you have twenty minutes.
>
> **[ Open TradeAid ]**
>
> The market is a skill, not a slot machine. Small reps, every session —
> that is the entire secret.
>
> — The TradeAid team

### Sign-in notice — template feel

> Subject: New sign-in to your TradeAid account
>
> Otto,
>
> Your account was signed in from a new session about a minute ago.
>
> If that was you, no action needed.
>
> If it wasn't, secure your account immediately: **[ Review activity ]**
>
> — The TradeAid team

Notice: no exclamation, no emoji, no marketing language. It reads like a note
from a person who cares whether it's actually you.

## What this voice implies beyond copy

This voice frames everything downstream. Not commitments today — a compass.

- **Support:** replies within a day, in the same voice. No canned "we appreciate
  your patience" openers. If we messed up, we say we messed up.
- **Marketing:** long-form over ads. Essays over hooks. We show up where
  serious learners already are, not where impressions are cheapest.
- **Hiring:** job listings read like this file. Interviews assume the candidate
  is competent — same respect we give the reader.
- **Pricing:** legible. No dark patterns. No "3 people just signed up" popups.
- **Product decisions:** if a feature would work best as an FOMO push, we don't
  build it. If it would work by making the reader smarter, we do.
